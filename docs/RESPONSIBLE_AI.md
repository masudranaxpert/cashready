# Responsible AI, fairness, and governance

CashReady operates as an advisory decision-support system for retail Mobile Financial Services (MFS) liquidity planning. It enforces data protection, algorithmic fairness, and human oversight.

## Synthetic data and regulatory compliance

To comply with customer banking secrecy standards and Bangladesh Bank Mobile Financial Services Regulations (2022), this prototype contains no customer Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or proprietary upay core banking records.

- Datasets are generated by `cashready/simulate.py` using assumed (illustrative) parameters; see cashready/config.py, alongside public calendar structures and macro-financial bounds.
- The simulation models 300 agents across 12 geographic areas with lognormal transaction amounts, Poisson arrival rates, weekly remittance flows, market haat days, salary cycles, and festival spikes (Eid-ul-Fitr).
- Causal time separation is enforced across partitions: training on days 0 to 49, validation on days 50 to 59, and held-out evaluation on days 60 to 89.

## Privacy and data protection

- External LLM isolation: Financial balances, transaction frequencies, and liquidity plans remain inside the local VPC or on-premise infrastructure. No operational data is transmitted to external language model APIs.
- Deterministic explanations: Explanations are calculated with TreeExplainer SHAP and rendered through deterministic bilingual templates (Bangla and English) to avoid hallucinated recommendations.
- Tokenized identifiers: Agents and territories use synthetic identifiers (such as `T0001` or `A01`), with no connection to national IDs, phone numbers, or residential addresses.
- Data minimization: Nightly feature engineering aggregates transaction counts, velocities, and volume totals without retaining counterparty phone numbers or recipient identities.

## Algorithmic fairness and sub-population parity

Algorithmic liquidity recommendations must not penalize low-volume rural agents or newly onboarded agents with limited history.

### Geographic fairness across area types

| Area Type | Weight | Mean True Demand (Daily) | P50 Forecast MAE | Relative Error | Coverage (P10 to P90) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Urban Market | 25% | ~54,200 BDT | 2,873.5 BDT | 5.30% | 85.2% |
| Peri-Urban | 35% | ~39,800 BDT | 2,314.8 BDT | 5.82% | 84.9% |
| Rural Haat | 40% | ~26,400 BDT | 1,644.6 BDT | 6.23% | 85.0% |

Relative forecast error remains between 5.3% and 6.2% across all three environments, giving rural agents comparable forecast precision to urban agents.

### Cold-start and new agent handling

During days 30 to 60, 15 agents (5% of the network) onboarded with no prior transaction history.

To prevent erratic predictions from sparse logs, new agents receive an opening recommendation based on the area cluster median demand with a conservative confidence adjustment. The API flags `is_new: true` on low-confidence plans so distributors can provide targeted buffer support.

## Human agency and oversight

CashReady provides decision support rather than automated capital execution.

- Agents retain full control over physical cash stocking decisions.
- Risk settings offer three operational levels: 80% (higher risk, lower working capital), 90% (balanced baseline), and 95% (conservative, suited for high-volume booths or salary days).
- Each recommendation presents top-3 operational drivers (such as haat day demand or prior week cash-outs) so agents can review the underlying reasons.

## Escalation and uncertainty protocols

- Stock-out uncertainty: When predicted cash stock-out probability is between 0.40 and 0.60, the system flags the recommendation for agent confirmation (`ask agent to confirm`).
- Area alerts: When more than 30% of agents in a cluster are projected to stock out on the same day, the system alerts the Area Territory Manager to coordinate distributor float transfers.
- Out-of-distribution limits: If a predicted need exceeds 3.5 times the agent's 30-day moving average, the system caps the recommendation and flags the record for review.

## Known limitations

- Connectivity dependencies: The model assumes cellular data connectivity is active. Network outages prevent real-time data sync.
- Capital limits: CashReady computes liquidity demand. Agents with insufficient working capital require micro-credit or distributor credit lines to fulfill the recommendation.
- Unobserved local events: Unplanned local events (such as political rallies or road closures) fall outside log-based features without external event feeds.
